# Implementation Roadmap

## Track 1 — Complete Payment Core
Payment methods, payment attempts, provider capability matrix, hosted checkout, embedded checkout, webhook ingress and complete payment state synchronization.

## Track 2 — Financial Core
Ledger posting, balance projections, fees, reserves, settlement ingestion, payout eligibility/execution, bank abstraction and reconciliation.

## Track 3 — Money lifecycle
Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, evidence and accounting effects.

## Track 4 — Merchant product
Payment Links, dashboard, reports, admin, onboarding/KYC/KYB, risk.

## Track 5 — Developer platform
API keys, OAuth, scopes, webhook registration/signing/retry/replay/DLQ, SDKs and documentation.

## Track 6 — Integrations
Payment providers, banks, KYC providers, accounting/ERP connectors.

## Track 7 — Advanced
Mandates, virtual accounts, connected accounts, split payments and POS.

## Track 8 — Production hardening
Security/PCI scope, penetration tests, load tests, HA, backups, DR, RPO/RTO, monitoring, alerting, incident response and regulatory review.
